SERVICE POLICY

At Mr. Wilnot House Cleaning LLC, our mission is to provide clean, safe, and professional cleaning services with respect, honesty, and quality.

  • 1. Customers must schedule cleaning appointments in advance.
  • 2. Any cancellation or rescheduling should be made at least 24 hours before the appointment.
  • 3. Customers must provide access to the home, office, or property at the scheduled time.
  • 4. We are not responsible for items that are not properly secured before cleaning.
  • 5. For safety reasons, dangerous chemicals, weapons, or hazardous materials must be removed before service.
  • 6. Payment must be made as agreed before or after the service.
  • 7. Extra services may require an additional fee.
  • 8. Our team respects your home, privacy, and personal belongings.
  • 9. We reserve the right to refuse service if the environment is unsafe.
  • 10. Customer satisfaction is important to us. Please contact us if there is any concern after service.
